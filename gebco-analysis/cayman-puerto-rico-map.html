    Inside the Cayman Trough

    The deepest point of the Caribbean, and the most under-surveyed feature on this map. It's a ~347 km pull-apart basin on the North America–Caribbean plate boundary, straddled by the Mid-Cayman Rise — Earth's deepest seafloor-spreading center, home to the Beebe and Von Damm vent fields.
