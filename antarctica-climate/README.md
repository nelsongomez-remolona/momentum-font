# antarctica-climate

Anomaly analysis of two open Antarctic climate datasets.

- **[analyze_climate.py](./analyze_climate.py)** — `python3 analyze_climate.py` (needs `numpy`)
- **data/seaice/** — NSIDC Sea Ice Index v4.0, monthly SH extent, 1979– ([G02135](https://nsidc.org/data/g02135))
- **data/reader/** — SCAR READER station surface-air-temperature monthly means ([READER](https://legacy.bas.ac.uk/met/READER/))

Findings are written up in the top-level **[../ANTARCTICA_INSIGHTS.md](../ANTARCTICA_INSIGHTS.md)**.

Data are redistributed for reproducibility; original values unmodified.
Sea Ice Index: Fetterer et al., NSIDC. READER: Turner et al. / SCAR.
